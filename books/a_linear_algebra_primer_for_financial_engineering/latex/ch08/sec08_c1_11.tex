\section{Ordinary least squares for random variables}

The ordinary least squares problem for random variables requires finding the best
approximation of a random variable by a linear combination of other random variables
and a constant.

We look for the best approximation of $Y$ by a linear combination of $X_1, X_2, \ldots,
X_n$ plus a constant, i.e., we look for constants $a, b_1, b_2, \ldots, b_n$ such that
\[
Y \;-\; \left(a + \sum_{i=1}^n b_iX_i\right) \quad \text{has mean 0 and minimal
variance.}
\]
