Let $F = Se^{(r-q)T}$ be the forward price of the asset at time $T$. Then,
$Se^{-qT} = Fe^{-rT}$ and the Put--Call parity (8.22) can be written as
\begin{equation}
C - P \;=\; Fe^{-rT} - Ke^{-rT}.
\end{equation}

Denote the discount factor by
\begin{equation}
disc \;=\; e^{-rT},
\end{equation}
and the present value of the forward price by
\begin{equation}
PVF \;=\; Fe^{-rT}.
\end{equation}

Then, (8.23) is the same as
\begin{equation}
C - P \;=\; PVF - K \cdot disc.
\end{equation}
